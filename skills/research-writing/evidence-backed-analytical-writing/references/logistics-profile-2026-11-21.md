# Logistics report profile (2026-11-21)

Use only when the brief calls for this form. Keep explicit numbered points such as
“一是/二是/三是”. If balanced length matters and no tighter target is given, use 10–15%
as a drafting tolerance, not a reason to add filler. Keep recommendations in the
project's required voice and vocabulary.

To explain an equipment claim, identify what the equipment changes in staffing,
process, visibility, or reach, then state the resulting logistics effect and its
conditions. Prefer fleet or program aggregates when asked for summary data; use
individual cases only when their measured result carries the point. Separate equipment
orders and trials from actual inventory or sustained use.

If the brief specifies light sourcing (简单出处), name the outlets inline and end with
a single `来源：` line naming the outlets and year window. Keep numbered citations in
working records only. On conversion, check for no residual numbered markers, a source
line, and a complete closing sentence.
