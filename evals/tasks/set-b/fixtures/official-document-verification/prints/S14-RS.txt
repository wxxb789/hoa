DOCK RELIABILITY PACKAGE

TITLE III. STATIONS
Sec. 301. Station recordkeeping.
Monthly station tide totals and lost-receipt counts are due to the board.
Sec. 382. Finance of pier maintenance.
The schedule adopted pursuant to Island Docks Act 8(b) shall identify any balance
available for pier surface repairs. No dates in that Act are amended.

TITLE IV. INCIDENTS
Sec. 415. Deck Relay.
The board shall count incident notices mentioning Deck Relay and publish the
aggregate count each January. This section confers no exemption or waiver.
Sec. 419. Incident retention.
Incident record retention shall not be shorter than two years.

TITLE IX. REGISTRATION
Sec. 904. Registration sunset.
The temporary bidder registration form expires September 30, 2031.
Sec. 907. Annual return.
The registrar shall circulate the revised return to all stations.
