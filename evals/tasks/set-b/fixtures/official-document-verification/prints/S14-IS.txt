DOCK RELIABILITY PACKAGE

Sec. 301. Station recordkeeping.
Monthly station tide totals are due to the board.
Sec. 415. Deck Relay.
The director may waive the fixed shore relay requirement upon a written request
from an operator using a Deck Relay on a seasonal route.
Sec. 904. Procurement index.
The registrar shall standardize the index of procurement bids.
