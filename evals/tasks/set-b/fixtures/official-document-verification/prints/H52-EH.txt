DOCK RELIABILITY PACKAGE

TITLE I. ISLAND INFRASTRUCTURE
Sec. 110. Receipt of navigation records.
The board shall archive the station's tide ledgers in electronic form.
Sec. 126. Continuity of island pier service.
In Island Docks Act 8(b), strike "March 31, 2029" and insert "March 31, 2032".
Sec. 134. Dispatch exception for remote piers.
An inspector may waive the fixed shore relay requirement for a Deck Relay at
a pier served fewer than twelve days per quarter, subject to a written decision.

TITLE IX. ADMINISTRATIVE CONTRACTS
Sec. 903. Form of bid security.
The registrar may accept a standing surety bond.
Sec. 904. Sunset for bundled contracts.
In Municipal Purchasing Act 4(d), replace the sunset with September 30, 2031.
Sec. 905. Transition.
Existing bids shall be evaluated under the rules in effect when received.

TITLE XII. STATION FUND
Sec. 1202. Training allowances.
Allowances are available for the registration of new pilot instructors.
