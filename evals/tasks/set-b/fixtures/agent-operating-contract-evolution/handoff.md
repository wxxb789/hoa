# Owner handoff

Archive output with an untraceable source is turning up in all three working
locations. I want attribution to hold wherever work starts, including when
catalogers draft material that a parent will deliver later. A cataloger only
drafts entries: it neither talks to me nor persists a delivery. Keep its brief
limited to work it actually does.

| ID | Rule under discussion |
|---|---|
| ATTR | Every claim in a delivery-bound entry carries its accession source, or is explicitly marked unsourced and withheld from delivery. |
| TONE | Use short, decisive wording when reporting directly to the owner. |
| SYNC | Only the persistence job publishes or commits the store. |

Old onboarding says the checkout overlay supplements the protocol and that
the nightly sync blocks orphan links. Please do not rely on that sheet alone.
