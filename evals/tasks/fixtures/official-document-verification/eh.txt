TITLE IX. PORT SERVICES
SEC. 904. SHORELINE SUBSIDY REPORTING.
The Secretary shall publish a final accounting of claims under section 18 of the Harbor Services Act by September 30, 2031.

SEC. 911. TRANSITION RULE.
Payments under section 18 of the Harbor Services Act may be made after September 30, 2029, solely for claims approved on or before that date.
