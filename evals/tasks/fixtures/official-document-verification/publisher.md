# H.R. 4382 retrieval note

The press index entry for August 12 reads: "Section 904 continues the
shoreline subsidy into 2031." Its permalink has no print suffix.

The two text files are publisher full-text captures. In existing law,
section 18(b) ends eligibility for new claims on September 30, 2029. Report
deadlines and payment of approved claims do not change eligibility.

These are legislative prints, not enacted law. Capture fingerprints appear
in the separate print registry.
